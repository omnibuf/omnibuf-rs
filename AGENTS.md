# Project Rules

- **NEVER** use `"We're still paying for"` or any variation such as `"still paying for"`, `"paying the price"`, or `"pay the cost"`.
- **NEVER** use em-dashes anywhere in documentation, comments, or code.
- Avoid formulaic sentence openers (`"Because every..."`, `"For projects that..."`, `"With .proto files..."`, `"Like Protobuf..."`), instructional `"If you..., you can..."` / `"When you..."` framing, `"instead of / rather than / without [gerund]"` clauses, 2-sentence `"X does A. It also does B."` paragraph templates, parenthetical taxonomy titles, and repetitive FAQ cadences. Vary sentence lengths sharply across technical prose.
